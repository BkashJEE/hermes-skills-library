# Code of conduct

## Our standards

We want this project to be a welcoming place for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Helpful participation includes:

- giving specific, respectful feedback;
- assuming good intent while addressing concrete behavior;
- crediting source authors and maintainers accurately;
- protecting private information and credentials; and
- accepting correction when evidence changes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, or repeatedly disrupting project work.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, project discussions and public spaces where someone is representing this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary project disagreements, use the relevant issue or pull request and keep the discussion technical. For harassment or another sensitive conduct concern, contact the maintainer through a private channel listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/BkashJEE). Do not place sensitive reports in a public issue.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when needed to protect the community. Reports will be reviewed as privately and fairly as possible.
